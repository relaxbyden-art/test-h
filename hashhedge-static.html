QUESTIONS

The honest FAQ.

Everything we'd want to know before giving a prop firm our money.

Where can I get detailed information about how the platform works?

Everything from purchasing a challenge to withdrawing profits is in our User Guide.

What payment methods are available?

We accept USDT only, on the following networks: TRC20 (Tron), ERC20 (Ethereum), BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain), Solana, Arbitrum, Optimism.

Why do I have to pay for the challenge?

The fee covers access to the trading platform and evaluation of your skills. In return you get a chance to trade with up to $200,000 and keep 80% of the profit without risking your own money.

What rules must I follow during a challenge?

Stay within the daily and maximum drawdown limits and hit the profit target within the required number of trading days. Details for each package are on the purchase page and in the User Guide.

What happens if I exceed the loss limit?

The challenge is permanently locked. Doesn't matter which limit, daily or overall. Unrealized losses count too.

How is the Daily Loss limit calculated?

Daily Loss = Current Equity − Balance at the beginning of the day. Open positions affect the calculation. The limit resets at 00:13 UTC+4. Unrealized profits/losses carry forward to the next day. Detailed breakdown with examples here.

What is a trading day?

A day on which at least one position was opened. If you open on Monday and close on Wednesday, only Monday counts.

What is Settlement Time and when does it occur?

Daily calculation of your account results. Happens at the end of the trading day in UTC+4. Balances are finalized and limits are updated.

Which assets can I trade?

160+ crypto assets, metals (gold, silver, platinum, palladium), oil and natural gas. Full list with position limits here.

Is spot trading available?

No. Futures only.

What is the maximum leverage I can trade with?

Up to 1:5.

Can I trade using different margin modes simultaneously?

No. One mode at a time: Cross or Isolated. To switch, close all positions in the current mode first.

Can I hedge positions?

Yes. You can open positions in opposite directions on the same asset.

Can I hold open positions for several days?

Yes. But watch your limits – open positions are factored into drawdown calculations.

Do open positions affect loss limits?

Yes. Unrealized P&L directly affects Equity and drawdown calculations. You can breach a limit without closing a position.

Is using a Stop Loss mandatory?

No. But we recommend it to help you stay within drawdown limits. More on TP/SL setup here.

Can I trade during weekends or important news events?

Yes. No restrictions.

What are the platform fees?

Details on fees and funding rates here.

Can I participate in multiple challenges simultaneously?

Yes, on stages 1 and 2 you can run multiple challenges in parallel. On stage 3 (funded) you can only trade one challenge per account size.

I've completed the challenge. What's next?

You get a funded account (accumulative account). Trade with our capital, keep 80% of the profit.

Are funded account conditions different from evaluation?

No. Drawdowns, leverage, execution, fees – everything is the same across all stages. What you see on evaluation is what you get on funded.

Is there a consistency rule or a daily profit cap?

No. No requirement to spread profit evenly across days. No cap on how much you can make in a single day. Hit the target within the minimum trading days and you move on.

Is there an extra fee to activate the funded account?

No. The challenge fee is the only payment. Funded account activates at no additional cost.

How do I reach support?

Website chat (icon in the bottom right), email support@hashhedge.com, Telegram bot @hashhedgesupportbot. Support is available 24/7.
